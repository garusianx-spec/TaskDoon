# Public website and authentication direction

Mode: Persuade for marketing; Operate for authentication. User-confirmed, code-first.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show the shared workspace, then let a visitor turn a conversation into a task and
track it in a project. Product clarity and ownership lead; personal productivity is secondary.

OWN-WORLD: Existing IRANYekan typography, indigo #3538CD, white public surfaces, semantic
status colors, disciplined RTL spacing, and the application's actual shared controls.
No customer proof, pricing promises, or product capabilities are invented.

STORY: Understand the product in one viewport, try the conversation-to-project mechanism,
review real capabilities and published CMS content, then enter the continuous OTP-first journey.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Responsive navigation, a centered concise Persian headline, two clear CTAs,
then a wide authentic workspace preview. Mobile replaces the shrunken desktop screenshot
with a readable task-focused composition. Authentication pairs a focused form with an
indigo product panel on desktop; mobile shows only the task and essential navigation.

FORM: Surface seed `6aafc33b`, dealt indices 4, 2, 1. The user chose the wide workspace
composition (4) and the conversation-to-task walkthrough from (2). The decision page was
unreachable through the browser boundary; the user explicitly selected the composition in chat.
The signature interaction uses real shared controls and editable demonstration state, clearly
labeled as sample data. Motion acknowledges tab/state changes and never hides primary content.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review,
the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
